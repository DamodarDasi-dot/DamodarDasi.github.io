How the unit works

A portable fueling unit that combines storage, dispensing and smart fleet management in one package: the tank is loaded by its own loading pump, monitored by an intelligent ATG, and every fill is authorised and recorded.

The animation follows the unit through loading, tank gauging, driver and vehicle authorisation, dispensing, real-time transaction records, and the venting and drain provisions on the double-wall tank.
