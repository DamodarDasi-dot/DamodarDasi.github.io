What I Integrate

DESIGNED: 3D structural and mechanical arrangements around equipment, piping and package interfaces.

PERFORMED: Equipment positioning, support arrangement, access considerations and interface definition within the overall package.

REVIEWED: Detailed manufacturing drawings produced by team members where the project required delegated drafting.

Engineering Focus
