02

Typical Engineering Scope

DESIGNED: RFQ-based mechanical package arrangements, tank/equipment layouts and 3D concepts for the proposed fueling systems.

PERFORMED: Mechanical interface definition for filling, dispensing, draining, venting, vapor recovery, instrumentation and safety equipment.

COORDINATED: Design requirements across equipment, instrumentation, electrical and package interfaces as required by the RFQ.

Tank formatsSkid-mounted · Portable · Mobile
Fuel serviceDiesel · Petrol
Typical controlsATG · Overfill · E-stop
SafetyVenting · Leak detection · Fire protection
03

Design Features Across RFQs

Fuel Handling

Fill connections, dispensing connections, drains, tank access, dip measurement and product-handling interfaces.

Vapor Management

Breather and vapor-recovery arrangements, including loading vapor-recovery interfaces where specified.

Instrumentation

ATG consoles/probes, overfill protection, leak sensing and project-specific level/alarm interfaces.

Safety

Emergency shutdown, emergency venting, fire/gas detection where specified, extinguishers and safety interlocks.

Electrical Interface

Package electrical/control-panel interface requirements and equipment coordination based on the RFQ.

Fabrication Readiness

Design development intended to support subsequent procurement, manufacturing and fabrication release after PO.

04

Engineering Workflow

RFQ→Requirement Review→Concept / 3D Layout→Equipment Interfaces→2D / GA→MTO / BOM→Quotation / Submission→PO→Manufacturing Release

Important Portfolio Status

F-02 through F-06 are presented as RFQ / pre-award engineering projects. The designs were completed against the respective RFQ requirements, but these projects were awaiting PO / manufacturing release and are therefore not presented as delivered or manufactured units.

Exact customer-controlled drawings and confidential RFQ documents are not reproduced on this public portfolio page.
