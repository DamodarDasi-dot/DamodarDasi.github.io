My Contribution

DESIGNED: Complete 3D package arrangement for all three machines in SolidWorks, with AutoCAD 2D drawings. Scope covered process piping, intercoolers, lubrication systems/equipment, supports, ETB piping, coupling guard/couplings and package interfaces.

PERFORMED: Stress calculations for the separately mounted lubrication skid and equipment/load calculations used for civil concrete-foundation design.

REVIEWED: Team manufacturing drawings and detailed design work. Participated in customer design reviews, query resolution, revisions and as-built documentation.

COORDINATED: Instrumentation selection/interfaces, customer requirements, China manufacturing support and FAT activities.

03

Package Architecture

03.1 · MAC

Main Air Compressor

Designed process piping, intercooler integration, lubrication piping and equipment including cooler, heater, auxiliary pumps, oil demisters and filters, plus package supports.

03.2 · BACKUP

Backup / Booster Compressor

Designed and integrated the backup machine arrangement and associated piping/equipment. The backup machine shares a common motor arrangement with the ETB expander.

03.3 · ETB

Expander Integration

Designed process piping and supports and integrated the coupling/guard arrangement with the common motor interface.

03.4 · LUBE

Shared Lubrication

Integrated shared lubrication piping and tank for the expander and backup machine. The lubrication skid included coolers, heaters, demisters and filters.

03.5 · STRUCTURAL

Skid & Foundation Interfaces

Performed lubrication-skid stress calculations and prepared equipment/load information for civil concrete foundation design. The machines themselves were treated as free-standing equipment.

03.6 · INTERFACES

Instrumentation & Tie-ins

Coordinated instrumentation selection, customer tie-in points, overall plant/package layout, customer reviews and manufacturing interfaces.

04

Execution Flow

Requirements→3D common arrangement→Process piping→Lube systems→Supports / interfaces→Drawings→Customer review→Manufacturing→FAT→As-built
05

Engineering Takeaway

This package required integration of three machines, shared auxiliary systems and multiple engineering interfaces within a common arrangement. My contribution combined hands-on 3D package design with lubrication-skid stress calculations, foundation-load engineering, multidisciplinary coordination, drawing review and manufacturing/FAT support.
