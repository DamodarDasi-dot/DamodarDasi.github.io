Engineering Focus

Tank Geometry

Developing practical tank geometry and arrangement within the available footprint and service requirements.

Interfaces

Defining nozzles, drains, vents, access openings and equipment interfaces for downstream package integration.

Manufacturability

Translating the 3D design into clear 2D fabrication information and coordinating drawing requirements with production.

Package Integration

Considering pumps, instruments, piping, skids and customer connection points as part of the overall mechanical system.
