02

Design Basis & Equipment

The package was developed around the specified nitrogen production, purity and delivery requirements. Feed-air quality was specified to ISO 8573-1 [2:4:1], with a −40°C pressure dew point requirement and ambient conditions up to 45°C.

The documented equipment selection included an oil-free VSD compressor, aftercooler, moisture separator, pre-filter, heatless desiccant dryer, after-filter, compressed-air receiver, PSA generator, nitrogen buffer, oxygen analyser, ODM, PSVs and ESD/interlock functions.

Feed-air design requirement~830 Nm³/h
Selected compressor capacity~900 Nm³/h
Air receiver12 m³
N₂ buffer25 m³
