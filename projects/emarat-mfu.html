03

Tank & Fuel-System Architecture

03.1

Dual-Compartment Tank

42,600 L gross capacity with 40,500 L safe-fill capacity, 6 mm inner and outer walls and a 75 mm fire-guard insulation arrangement.

03.2

Pressure & Testing

Design pressure 7 kPa and leak pressure test 21 kPa. Weld coefficient 0.85; inspection and welding references included NB/T 47013 and NB/T 47015.

03.3

Fuel Connections

Package included 3 in fill, 1.5 in bowser, 2 in product delivery, 2 in drain, vapor-recovery connections and breather/emergency vent arrangements.

03.4

Fueling Equipment

Integrated dispenser, submersible turbine pump, ATG console/probe, overfill protection, interstitial leak sensing and associated fueling interfaces.

03.5

Safety Systems

Explosion-proof control panel/lighting, static earthing, emergency ventilation, interstitial and primary emergency venting, spill/drainage provisions and fire-safety equipment.

03.6

Standards / References

The drawing package references UL 142 and UL 2085 for tank design/build/test/certification, with ASME B16.5 Class 150 flanges and other project-specific inspection/welding requirements.
