03

How the package works

An HV motor drives the bull gear of an integral gearbox, and two high-speed pinions carry the four impellers, two per pinion. Natural gas is compressed from 13.21 to 50.8 bar(a), with water-cooled coolers after every stage.

Dry gas seals (API 692) on each impeller shaft keep the gas in the casing, using filtered process gas with nitrogen as backup at standstill. At low flow the anti-surge valve recycles gas through its own water-cooled recycle cooler back to suction.

06

Key design challenges

05.1

Minimal footprint

Fitting four stages, coolers, lube-oil and seal-gas systems and instrument racks into a tight skid while keeping clearance around every item, down to small valves and instruments, for maintenance access and machine realignment.

05.2

Noise hood without moving tie-points

Designing the enclosure around the finished package without changing any customer tie-point, and agreeing each interface with the customer, including the case for an extra 500 mm to place the hood.

05.3

Seal and instrument routing

Routing seal-gas, vent and instrument lines for all four stages to the integrally geared machine so they stay short, drainable and clear of the main process piping.

05.4

Cooler and cooling-water integration

Placing the intercoolers, after cooler, recycle cooler and oil cooler, with their cooling-water supply and return piping, inside the hood while keeping access for maintenance.
