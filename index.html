About

I turn engineering requirements into equipment packages that can be fabricated, tested and installed.

My work sits between mechanical design, package integration and project execution: complete 3D package models, equipment arrangement, process and utility piping, lube-oil and seal systems, base frames and structural skids, GA and fabrication drawings, MTO/BOM — then the coordination that carries a design through procurement, manufacturing and FAT/SAT.

I spent four-plus years on Atlas Copco centrifugal compressor, compander and PSA nitrogen packages for international oil & gas and industrial projects, where I led and trained a team of six designers. Since November 2025 I've been in the UAE at Meta Green, designing mobile and skid-mounted fuel systems and storage tanks, and leading design on the Emarat 40 FT mobile fueling unit from tender to SAT.

From requirement to FAT

The sequence every package goes through, and where my work sits in it.

  1. Requirement

    RFQ, datasheets and customer specifications

  2. Design basis

    Duty, codes, interfaces and constraints fixed

  3. Equipment selection

    Sizing inputs and vendor coordination

  4. 3D design

    Full package model in SolidWorks

  5. Piping & integration

    Process, lube, seal and instrument piping

  6. Drawings

    GA, fabrication and foundation drawings

  7. MTO / BOM

    Quantities and specifications for purchasing

  8. Manufacturing

    Shop queries, revisions and fabrication support

  9. FAT / SAT

    Testing, inspection and punch-point closure
