About

Most of my projects start with a client spec and finish with equipment that has been built, tested and installed. I own the design, and I also handle the coordination needed to get it built.

On a typical package I do the engineering and the project coordination: the full 3D model, equipment arrangement, process and utility piping, lube-oil and seal systems, base frames and skids, GA and fabrication drawings, and the MTO/BOM. After that I follow the design through procurement, manufacturing and FAT/SAT.

I spent four-plus years at DesignTech Systems on centrifugal compressor, compander and PSA nitrogen packages for international oil & gas and industrial projects, where I led and trained a team of six designers. Since November 2025 I've been in the UAE at Meta Green, designing mobile and skid-mounted fuel systems and storage tanks, and leading design on the Emarat 40 FT mobile fueling unit from tender to SAT.
