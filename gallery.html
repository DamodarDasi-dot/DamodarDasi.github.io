Publication Checklist

SAFE TO PUBLISH: Your own photographs, your own original non-confidential illustrations, generic engineering diagrams, and drawings cleared for public use.

REDACT BEFORE PUBLISHING: customer names, project numbers, internal drawing numbers, confidential dimensions, proprietary equipment data, company logos and controlled document identifiers when required.

DO NOT PUBLISH: confidential Atlas Copco drawings, customer-controlled documents, proprietary calculations or internal documentation unless you have explicit permission.
